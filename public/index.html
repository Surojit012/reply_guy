🚀 How do I get started with Reply Guy? ▼

Getting started is simple:

  1. Paste any tweet text into the input field above
  2. Choose your preferences (length, style, tone)
  3. Click "Generate Reply" to create your response
  4. Copy the generated reply and paste it on Twitter

For even easier use, install our Chrome extension for direct Twitter integration!

🔧 How do I install the Chrome extension? ▼

Installing the extension is easy:

  1. Click the "Install Chrome Extension" button above
  2. Download the ZIP file from the installation page
  3. Extract the ZIP file to a folder on your computer
  4. Open Chrome and go to chrome://extensions/
  5. Enable "Developer mode" (toggle in top right)
  6. Click "Load unpacked" and select the extracted folder

No Chrome Web Store account needed - it's completely free!

🤖 How does the AI work? ▼

Reply Guy uses advanced AI technology:

  • Analysis: AI reads and understands the original tweet's context, tone, and purpose
  • Generation: Creates contextually appropriate replies based on your preferences
  • Model: Powered by Mistral AI through OpenRouter for high-quality responses
  • Privacy: Your tweets and replies are not stored or logged

The AI is trained to be helpful, respectful, and engaging in all responses.

🔒 Is my data safe and private? ▼

Your privacy is our priority:

  • No Data Storage: We don't store your tweets or generated replies
  • No Account Required: Use the service without creating an account
  • Secure Processing: All requests are processed securely and deleted immediately
  • No Tracking: We don't track your usage patterns or personal information

Your tweets are only used temporarily to generate replies, then immediately discarded.

❌ What if I get an error or the service isn't working? ▼

Common issues and solutions:

  • "Rate limit exceeded": Wait a minute and try again
  • "Failed to generate": Check your internet connection and retry
  • Extension not working: Make sure it's properly installed and enabled
  • No response: Try refreshing the page and generating again

If problems persist, try using a different browser or clearing your browser cache.
