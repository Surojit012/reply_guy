🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 30, 2024

Overview

Reply Guy is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information when you use our website and Chrome extension.

Information We Don't Collect

We believe in privacy by design. Here's what we DON'T do:

  • We don't collect personal information
  • We don't track your browsing behavior
  • We don't store your tweets or generated replies
  • We don't use cookies for tracking
  • We don't sell or share any data with third parties

How Your Data is Handled

Website Version:

  • Your OpenRouter API key is securely stored on our server
  • Tweet content is processed through our secure backend
  • We don't store your tweets or generated replies
  • All data is processed in real-time and not retained

Chrome Extension:

  • No API key required - uses our secure backend service
  • Your preferences are stored locally in your browser
  • Tweet content is sent to our server for AI processing only
  • No personal data is collected or stored

Third-Party Services

We use OpenRouter API for AI functionality:

  • Your tweets are sent to our server and then to OpenRouter for processing
  • We manage the API key securely on our backend
  • OpenRouter's privacy policy applies to their AI processing
  • No other third-party services have access to your data

Data Security

  • All communications use HTTPS encryption
  • No sensitive data is stored on our servers
  • Your browser handles all data storage locally

Chrome Extension Permissions

Our extension requests these permissions:

  • activeTab: To interact with Twitter pages and extract tweet text
  • storage: To save your preferences locally in your browser
  • your-domain.com: To communicate with our secure backend service

Your Rights

  • You can stop using our service at any time
  • You can delete the Chrome extension to remove all local data
  • You control your OpenRouter API key and can revoke it anytime

Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
